Programs

What We Offer

Every discipline under one roof, taught by professional coaches with decades of real-world experience.

MMA

Integrated MMA training that blends striking, takedowns, and grappling into a complete, competition-ready skillset.

Boxing

Foundational boxing training focused on footwork, head movement, combinations, and defensive technique for all skill levels.

Kickboxing

High-energy kickboxing classes combining punches, kicks, and knee strikes to build power, cardio, and full-body conditioning.

Jiu-Jitsu

Structured gi and no-gi Jiu-Jitsu classes that build control, submissions, and confidence on the ground.

Weight Training

Strength and conditioning programs built to develop functional power, endurance, and athleticism.

About Us

More Than a Gym

Amir Academy of Martial Arts is built on decades of real experience, discipline, and uncompromising standards. Founded on traditional values and evolved through modern combat sports, our academy develops confidence, character and physical excellence for students of all ages.

We don't follow trends. We build foundations.

We train kids, teens, and adults in Kickboxing, Jiu-Jitsu, and MMA, guided by professional instruction and decades of real-world experience.
